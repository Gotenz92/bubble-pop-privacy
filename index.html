Privacy Policy

Bubble Pop! Star Adventure · Last updated November 11, 2026

Bubble Pop! Star Adventure ("the app") is made by Chris Stimpfle. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Data saved on your device

Your game progress (such as scores, stars, and which bubble packs you have unlocked or selected) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes this data.

In-app purchases

Bubble packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out by opening an issue at github.com/Gotenz92/bubble-pop-privacy/issues.
